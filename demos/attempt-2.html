Conventions

Variants are data-* attributes named after the axis (ADR-0006); the default value's attribute is omitted. Below, the default combination -- data-tone="neutral", data-variant="secondary", no data-size (= md) -- renders from a completely bare element:

Every other swatch below states its tone/variant/size explicitly for legibility, even where that repeats the default. States are the platform's, not classes: this file adds data-force-state only so every state can be seen at once without you having to hover/click/tab through 200+ buttons -- the real :hover, :active and :focus-visible rules are also live on every enabled button above, and disabled swatches carry the real attribute.
